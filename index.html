04. Prototype: Technical 2D CAD & MEP Integration

Layer Control

Room size

Drag equipment, or click a block and use the arrow keys. Press R to rotate it and Ctrl+Z to undo. Drag a hood outline to move its cooking line; hoods resize to fit, and cooking units more than 1 m apart get separate hoods. Aisles under 900 mm are flagged between different zones. Equipment in the same zone, like the cooking line, is exempt, and so are neutral tables. Use one as a buffer between cold and hot equipment. A gap filled by another unit, like the bar top between underbar stations and stools, is not an aisle.

Drag drain, water and power points. Double-click a point to delete it.
