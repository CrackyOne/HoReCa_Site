Layer Control

Room size

Drag equipment, or focus a block and use the arrow keys. Drag the hood outline to move the whole cooking line; the hood resizes to fit it. Aisles under 900 mm are flagged between different zones. Equipment in the same zone, like the cooking line, is exempt, and so are neutral tables. Use one as a buffer between cold and hot equipment. A gap filled by another unit, like the bar top between underbar stations and stools, is not an aisle.

Drag drain, water and power points. Double-click a point to delete it.
